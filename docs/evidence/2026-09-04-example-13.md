## Proven limits

- A direct A2A message may have no context ID; the workflow reports continuation unavailable and does not manufacture one.
- Gateway policy remains role/scope/method based. This example does not claim gateway-enforced subject-to-route or context ownership.
- Agent Cards and all remote text are untrusted data, never trusted local instructions.
- Linux support is limited to x64 GNU systems with glibc 2.38 or newer; Windows and other upstream assets were not tested.
- Live wording is model-dependent. Assertions covered skill execution, workflow count, semantic store/recall, exact contexts, identity separation, attribution, and secret handling.
- The lab OAuth server, loopback HTTP, process-local ADK memory, and file-backed disposable secrets are not production identity or persistence designs.
